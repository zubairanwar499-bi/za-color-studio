How to use AIColors

1. Generate with AI: Enter any prompt in the search bar at the top (e.g. "Halloween warm whimsical" or "dark blue, green and red blush") and click Generate.

2. Browse Palettes: Choose from New (your session generation), Top (curated starter library), or History (your past creations) from the left navigation rail.

3. Instant Preview: Click on any palette in the sidebar. The central mockup will immediately repaint using those colors!

4. Export Colors: Click the Export button below the mockup to copy CSS variables, Tailwind classes, or JSON data to your clipboard.
